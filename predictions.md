## 2. My findings

One finding per problem.

### Finding 1 — Editing kitchen ingredients is not easy to access

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Recommendations, the “My kitchen” section and its Edit control.
- What I did, what I saw: I tried to change my selected ingredients while reviewing meals. The Edit control was not prominent, and making changes required returning to the setup flow.
- Which heuristic: #7 — Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: Screen. The app already supports editing ingredients; the entry point and interaction can be improved using existing functionality.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Users can complete the task, but finding the control and moving between screens adds unnecessary effort when adjusting their choices.
- The repair: Provide a prominent “Edit ingredients” control near the recommendations, allowing users to update their kitchen quickly while retaining their other settings.

### Finding 2 — Few recipes fully match the available ingredients

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Recommendations, ingredient-match results and the “Only meals I can cook now” filter.
- What I did, what I saw: I selected ingredients and reviewed the recommendations. Few recipes matched all my selected available ingredients without requiring additional items.
- Which heuristic: #2 — Match Between the System and the Real World; the recommendations should accommodate the ordinary kitchen situations the product is intended to support.
- Screen or system: System. Improving coverage requires changes to the recipe collection or matching capabilities; changing the screen alone cannot produce more suitable recipes.
- Severity, and why: 3 — Limited fully matched options can prevent users from completing the core task of finding something to cook without additional shopping.
- The repair: Expand coverage of common, simple meals and support appropriate ingredient substitutions, clearly distinguishing fully matched recipes from recipes that require changes.

### Finding 3 — Some ingredient emojis are misleading

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Setup, the ingredient picker, including the long bean option.
- What I did, what I saw: I browsed the ingredient options and noticed that some emojis did not clearly represent the named ingredients, such as long bean.
- Which heuristic: #2 — Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The ingredient names are already available; the visual representations can be replaced or removed without changing the matching logic.
- Severity, and why: 1 — The mismatch reduces visual clarity, but the accompanying text still allows users to identify the ingredient.
- The repair: Use recognisable, appropriate ingredient visuals and rely on clear text labels where no suitable emoji exists.

### Finding 4 — Expanded ingredient options overlap with the search bar

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Setup, the ingredient picker after expanding the ingredient list.
- What I did, what I saw: I expanded the ingredient list and found that the options at the top overlapped with the search bar, making the affected content harder to read.
- Which heuristic: #8 — Aesthetic and Minimalist Design.
- Screen or system: Screen. The problem concerns positioning, spacing and layering of existing interface elements.
- Severity, and why: 2 — The overlap obscures information and makes selection more difficult; this should rise to 3 if it prevents clicking an option or using search.
- The repair: Keep the search bar and ingredient options clearly separated and usable throughout expansion and scrolling across supported screen sizes.

### Finding 5 — Cooking instructions are positioned too late in the recipe details

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Detail, the section navigation and “How to cook it” section.
- What I did, what I saw: I opened a recipe to check how to prepare it. “How to cook it” appeared last in the section order, although a direct navigation option was available.
- Which heuristic: #7 — Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: Screen. The cooking instructions and section navigation already exist; their order and prominence can be changed without adding new data.
- Severity, and why: 2 — A central part of evaluating and using a recipe receives low priority, adding effort for users who want to check the method immediately.
- The repair: Position cooking instructions earlier or provide a prominent “View cooking steps” shortcut near the top of the recipe details.

### Finding 6 — Recipe details lack a finished-dish image

- Where: https://pantrypilot-phi.vercel.app — Meal Detail, the recipe header and overview.
- What I did, what I saw: I opened a recipe and found its name, emoji and written description, but no finished-dish image to help me understand what the meal should look like.
- Which heuristic: #6 — Recognition Rather than Recall.
- Screen or system: System. Recipe-specific image assets need to be supplied and associated with the recipes before the screen can display an accurate visual reference.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Users unfamiliar with a dish must infer its appearance from text, making it harder to judge whether they want to prepare it.
- The repair: Display an accurate, representative finished-dish image near the top of each recipe, alongside its name and description.
